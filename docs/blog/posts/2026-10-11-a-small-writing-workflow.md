# A small writing workflow that sticks

A writing habit is easier to keep when the process is simple. A short loop—capture, shape, draft, and publish—helps ideas move forward without requiring a perfect plan.

<!-- more -->

## Capture the idea

Keep a single inbox for possible topics. Write down the question, observation, or problem that sparked the idea, along with a sentence about why it might matter to someone else.

## Shape before drafting

Before writing full paragraphs, jot down the point you want readers to remember and a few supporting sections. If the outline is hard to explain, the idea may need more thought.

## Draft for clarity

Write the first pass to get the reasoning out. Then edit for the reader: remove repetition, explain unfamiliar terms, and make headings describe what each section delivers.

## Finish with a useful next step

End by giving the reader something concrete to try, consider, or explore. A useful article does not need to be long; it needs to make its main idea easy to understand and apply.
